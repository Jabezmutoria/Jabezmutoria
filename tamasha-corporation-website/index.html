About Us

Our Story

Tamasha Corporation Limited, based in Nairobi, Kenya, holds a vast catalogue of both local and international music rocking the airwaves. Over the years the company has interacted with the music of internationally known artists — Franco, Tabu Ley, Simba Wanyika, Bert Kaempfert, and Roger Whittaker, among many more.

We are situated at The Mall, Westlands — a landmark multi-tenanted shopping centre at the junction of Waiyaki Way and Ring Road, Westlands, Nairobi.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve and distribute the best of African music & cultural content to the world.

Distribute

Getting African music onto airwaves and platforms worldwide, electronically.

Protect

Royalties and rights management for the artists and catalogues we represent.

Connect

A Nairobi home base linking artists, partner labels, and the wider community.

Our Catalogue

Tamasha was formed through acquisition of the archive of Polygram Records East Africa when it divested from the region in the early 1990s — at the time the premier recording and publishing label in East Africa, holding a vast archive of over 10,000 tracks from the region's most loved artists of the 1950s–1980s.

Since then the archive has been digitized and made available across CD, streaming, digital download and mobile — spanning Zilizopendwa, Rhumba and Gospel among other genres.
